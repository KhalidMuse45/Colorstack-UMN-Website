## Open questions

1. **Chloe Jackman Photography credit.** All 12 files in `UMNSTACKEDUP/` are professional photography from the national Stacked Up Summit, credited to Chloe Jackman Photography in the filenames. Confirm usage rights for the chapter website before publishing any of them — this is third-party commercial photography, not a chapter member's own camera roll.
2. **Identifiable members named in filenames.** `MOD&AYUB`, `SALManSAID`, `Fiqi`, `Hangatu`, and `MUSE` name individuals directly. No names were used in alt text or descriptions above, per instructions. Chapter permission to publish is assumed per `assets-src/README.md`, but flagging explicitly since these are prominent, repeated, close-up appearances of a small number of people.
3. **`candids(fromboardshoots)/` is a styled fashion photoshoot, not meeting candids.** 42 of the 45 files show the same two or three board members in coordinated, editorial wardrobe at Northrop Auditorium locations (grand staircase, colonnade, "Presidents Circle" wall) or a whiteboard/computer-lab set piece — not an actual chapter meeting or event in progress. Confirm with e-board whether this is acceptable for "In the Room" (which the design doc frames as "this is what a Tuesday looks like") or whether the chapter wants genuine, less-staged meeting-night photos with broader member representation before that slot ships.
4. **No Community-program photos in this batch.** Nothing new shows a game night or a potluck; the incumbent `game-night-chess.jpg`/`game-night-signage.jpg` remain the only source. Worth adding to `docs/CONTENT-NEEDED.md` if not already tracked.
5. **Near-duplicate/burst-frame pairs in `UMNSTACKEDUP/`.** The "SCAN HERE" crowd scene, the yellow-couch conversation, and the Notio-booth and Duolingo-booth conversations each exist as two files (a `_websize` export and a full-resolution export, or two adjacent burst frames). Recommend picking one per scene and either dropping the redundant file or documenting why both are kept.
6. **`IMG_4221` is duplicated across two directories** (`UMNSTACKEDUP/` and `candids(fromboardshoots)/`, byte-identical). Recommend consolidating to a single location.
7. **`IMG_4152` is a square crop** (3000x3000) of what is otherwise a portrait-oriented shoot — confirm whether a re-crop to true portrait is wanted if this photo is selected for a portrait slot.
8. **Whiteboard typo.** `IMG_4227`/`IMG_4228` read "Leet Code Acedemic" (sic) on the whiteboard. If either is used for the Workshops slot, the misspelling will be legible in the final crop — confirm that's acceptable or that the crop can exclude it.
